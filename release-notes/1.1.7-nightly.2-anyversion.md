### What's new since v1.1.7-nightly.1

**Pre-1.9 servers**

- On servers older than 1.9 the bot clicks like a 1.8 player: about 10 clicks a second, no crit hops, and the arm swing goes out before the hit like a real 1.8 client (1be06b1, 45d7547)
- Swords are picked over axes there, since there's no cooldown or shields to make an axe worth it (afdc9de)

**Fighting**

- The bot turns onto mobs a few degrees a tick like a hand on a mouse, instead of snapping its head. `smoothCombatAim` off snaps again (4a248e8)
- Swings only go out when the crosshair is actually on the mob, with no block in the way, so no more hitting through walls or whiffing at air (5056951)
- Crit hops are timed right after switching weapons, and mobs that haven't hit you yet get the opener hop too (518f852)
- Kill tasks walk in closer and only back off from a mob right on top of them, so the back-off stops cancelling the hop (682a349)

**#gamer portal**

- The lava pour waits for the aim to sit still before clicking, and the bot scoops up its own lava spills instead of walking into them (c626a34)
- Building the obsidian frame one block at a time no longer breaks the obsidian it just placed (08d9946)
- A lit portal is recognised and walked into instead of building a second one. That second build could flood a huge lava lake and end the whole run (ce93da1)
- The cast is only given up on once the frame has stopped making progress for 3 minutes, not just on the clock (ce93da1)

**Mining**

- Bobbing in a small water pocket no longer restarts the wait to mine dry every hop (dfcecfb)

**Updates**

- The update card shows up on release jars now. Proguard broke it on the first nightly.1 download (ea4c2f5)

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs. If something acts weird, report it
- If you never saw the update card on nightly.1, your copy can't update itself. Download this one by hand once
