### What's new since v1.1.7-nightly.8

**#gamer is still being worked on**

One full test game beat the game on the bed fix below, but it's one world, and not our usual test world. It's the world that sat stuck for 45 min on the bed errand before: 0 deaths, in the End at 1 h 12 min, dragon dead with beds about 3 min later. That game didn't have the two small fixes at the bottom yet. Wins aren't reliable yet.

The finished wither skeleton fix got two test games on the two worlds that had 4 wither deaths between them. 0 wither deaths in the Nether in both, but neither beat the game:

- 170 min cap, 2 deaths (an enderman in the End, a skeleton in the overworld), back at the dragon, with the dragon still at full health, when time ran out
- 1 death to an enderman during the pearl hunt, then stuck 45 min on the bed errand with a full bag. That's the stall the bed fix below is for

**Wither skeleton packs**

- A pack of wither skeletons in the Nether now blocks off the floor it's standing on, so the bot won't drop down onto it from a ledge above. Hoglin packs got the same (15ee4da)
- Running away and picking blazes keep off those floors too. A blaze close to a pack gets skipped instead of chased into it (f01291a)
- At low health a run isn't over while any wither skeleton is within 20 blocks, even one that hasn't noticed it yet (15ee4da)
- The pack's wall is a real wall now. It used to try to dig through the empty air at the edge and swing at nothing until the move timed out (f01291a)

**Beds with a full bag**

- When it needs room for beds before the End, it throws the biggest stack of building blocks first. The bed throw never picks logs, planks, wool or beds, and it keeps the 48 blocks the End needs. It used to throw the oak log it was out getting (0ed6c8e)
- It frees two slots instead of one, and shears stay off leaves while it gets the beds, so the trees on the way don't fill the bag right back up (0ed6c8e)
- On the world that sat stuck before: 7 throws, the 6 missing beds in 14 s, 9 beds in the bag going into the End

**Small fixes, lab-tested**

- When you stop the bot mid-break, the breaking sound no longer keeps going. It now tells the server it stopped digging. Before this it kept the hit sound going 5 times a second until the test ended 3 minutes later (247e440, ca06e5d)
- With a pickaxe, gathering blocks for a path now digs the grass and dirt around you first, instead of walking off to stone further away (247e440)

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs
- `#gamer` is still being worked on and isn't reliable yet
- Endermen are still a problem: one Nether death during the pearl hunt, one in the End
- 4 wither hits in one game came before the first pack had been seen, so a pack it hasn't spotted yet can still hurt
- After the win, the bot kept shuffling around next to the exit portal instead of jumping in, until the test ended. Step in yourself
- If a run stops and says so, type `#gamer` and it picks back up
