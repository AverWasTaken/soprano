First Soprano release for Minecraft 1.21.11. It has everything from v1.1.4 on 26.3, plus the stuff below. The schematic builder and some other 26.3 v1.1.5 stuff (multi-ore `#get`, the builder and pathing fixes) aren't on this version yet.

### What's new and fixed

**New stuff**

- Auto torch: places torches in dark spots while mining, walking or doing tasks. Off by default (994f648)
- Self defense: when a mob hits it during `#mine`, `#tunnel`, `#build`, `#farm`, `#follow` or `#goto`, it pulls out its best sword or axe, fights back, then swaps back to what it was holding. On by default (ba440c7)
- When a path would be a lot faster if it collected some blocks first, it asks you. Answer with `#gather yes` or `#gather no`, or click Yes/No in chat (fe1d362)
- `#follow players` sticks to one player instead of flipping between them (e99c92b)
- Opens bastion chests for smithing templates instead of breaking them (30fe4ca)
- Raises its shield when two mobs pile on it (b95cd14)
- Stops running away from mobs that keep hitting it and fights back (71a86dc)

**Fixes**

- Gravel or sand that slid in front of a block gets broken first (59188f0)
- Nether portal builder picks spots it can actually reach (b51999f)
- Gets a diamond pickaxe before going through the portal (d0b0ab4)
- Blaze hunting goes to the blazes instead of camping (d4a1a68)
- Lava: being on fire on dry land no longer sends it swimming through lava, and cast lava only gets poured into a clear spot (6bfad26, a61590d)
- Swims up out of 2 deep water instead of pillaring (3f1262f)
- Braking in the middle of a sprint jump lets go of W or holds S, instead of spinning around (2a7368e)
- Get tab stops showing aliased items twice (abee17f)
- Fetches a whole wood job in one trip and stops throwing away a good pickaxe (bdbd281, b18fcf9)

**How to turn it on**

- Auto torch: `#autoTorch true` (or `#set autoTorch true`). Keep torches in your hotbar. `autoTorchLightLevel` (default 7) and `autoTorchRadius` (default 4) tune it
- Self defense is already on. `#selfDefense false` turns it off
- Never want to be asked about collecting blocks: `#askToGatherForPath false`

**Good to know**

- Not every new feature got play-tested on every version. If something acts weird, report it
