### What's new and fixed

**New stuff**

- Auto torch: places torches in dark spots while mining, walking or doing tasks. Off by default (8d5a014)
- Self defense: when a mob hits it during `#mine`, `#tunnel`, `#build`, `#farm`, `#follow` or `#goto`, it pulls out its best sword or axe, fights back, then swaps back to what it was holding. On by default (82ab5c0)
- When a path would be a lot faster if it collected some blocks first, it asks you. Answer with `#gather yes` or `#gather no`, or click Yes/No in chat (f75b184)
- `#follow players` sticks to one player instead of flipping between them (fc4ded0)
- `farmUsingSelection`: `#farm` only works the crops inside your selection. Off by default (1d484cb)
- Opens bastion chests for smithing templates instead of breaking them (e9f80c7)
- Raises its shield when two mobs pile on it (3f19c77)
- Stops running away from mobs that keep hitting it and fights back (d59b78e)

**Fixes**

- Sprint jumps up a step check the keys ahead of time, so it stops slamming into the step and losing speed (c8bd1c8)
- Backs up a little before a hop that starts too close to the step (0910736)
- Braking in the middle of a sprint jump lets go of W or holds S, instead of spinning around (b4d06b3)
- Long drops no longer end in "No path found", and a fall that hurts isn't treated as free anymore (6235fd1, 19e3a25)
- Boats row around islands instead of hopping out, and go straight across open water (f6ebd45, 6cb0386)
- `#food` stops flip-flopping between breaking and replanting crops (5c3c5f0)
- Tree chopping stops cycling through logs it can't reach (0576c01)
- Gravel or sand that slid in front of a block gets broken first (0a1c335)
- Nether portal builder picks spots it can actually reach (8cfafe2)
- Gets a diamond pickaxe before going through the portal (6b7c4ee)
- Blaze hunting goes to the blazes instead of camping (c6d72c0)
- Lava: being on fire on dry land no longer sends it swimming through lava, and cast lava only gets poured into a clear spot (f60443d, 620ce67)
- Swims up out of 2 deep water instead of pillaring (e968fd5)
- Fetches everything a job needs in one trip before crafting (906d8db)
- Night bed gets placed from the right spot (1f2d3d5)
- Get tab stops showing aliased items twice (f0df40a)

**How to turn it on**

- Auto torch: `#autoTorch true` (or `#set autoTorch true`). Keep torches in your hotbar. `autoTorchLightLevel` (default 7) and `autoTorchRadius` (default 4) tune it
- Farm only your selection: `#farmUsingSelection true`
- Boats are still off by default. `#allowBoats true` to let it use them
- Self defense is already on. `#selfDefense false` turns it off
- Never want to be asked about collecting blocks: `#askToGatherForPath false`

**Good to know**

- Backing up before a hop (`brakeAscends`) turns your head around for a tick or three. If the anticheat on your server cares, `#brakeAscends false`
- Not every new feature got play-tested on every version. If something acts weird, report it
