### What's new since v1.1.7-nightly.5

**#gamer beat the game again in testing**

Fresh random world to the credits in about 1 h 40 min with 0 deaths, on exactly this code. First Nether trip took 57 min with one food trip home, End at 1 h 36 min, dragon from 200 to 1 in 7 clicks for 2.9 self damage. That's 2 full wins across builds now, this one and one on nightly.5's code. It's still not reliable, see Good to know at the bottom

**The dragon and the End trip**

- The pillar bed goes down with the yaw the server actually has, so it faces the right way. A bed that still faces wrong gets broken, not clicked. In a test that starts at the End with a kit: 4 of 4 to the credits, 29 of 29 clicks facing east (08a5adf)
- Brings 16 eyes instead of 14, and waits 40 s instead of 20 for a dropped one. Being one short at the portal cost a whole Nether trip (be4d4ee)
- Tops food up to 56 in the prep before the dig, after the beds and the gear. The prep's 5 minute cap or a dry field ends it, 24 is still the hard line (13c05cb)
- Digs down from dry land to a far End portal instead of walking at whatever is straight above it, which could be a lake (7cb1ed3)

**The Nether**

- Walks in with 56 blocks instead of 128 and digs netherrack up to 128 at the first calm moment, so no more long dirt trip before the portal (7426453, ece66ed, 4f8dcee)
- Walks to real ground before fighting on a lava shore (1ae0fef)
- Stops parking next to lava waiting for a magma cube to come to it (edd52d9, 2c48596)
- A run from blazes now counts the ones that can see it out to 48 blocks instead of 20 (5a294e7)
- Fire touching the bot gets broken on the spot, the path and the fight keep going instead of standing still for it (fdc8ab5)
- Doesn't give up on rods below the minimum, and doesn't sweep away from a spawner it just picked (4669e29)
- The pearl hunt counts lava back offs by place: three within 32 blocks and a minute and it walks off. It also won't walk through that lava for loot (1e7b991)

**Fights**

- A run from skeletons no longer treats 12 blocks or a brief break in sight as enough space (b44fc50)
- Stands and fights a pillager patrol one at a time when it has armor and a sword or axe, running just took bolts in the back. Still runs around hp 10. Lab with waves of 5, 5 and 3 pillagers against an iron kit: 0 deaths (2cfa444)
- Doesn't walk up to a skeleton holding only a pickaxe (caa6e12)

**Wood, wool and vines**

- Doesn't cut down vines it planned to climb. In a lab tower 10 vines went to 8 and it gave up on the log, now all 10 stay and it has the log in about 10 s (9bdce41)
- Looks for the first night's sheep out to 128 blocks instead of 40, so the wool trip happens in daylight (9d44641)
- A sheep hunt already running keeps going when it leaves the crafting table behind (9d44641)
- A batch craft with two kinds of wood in the bag doesn't swap them on the slot forever, it crafts one at a time (b44fc50)
- A buried log stays buried once it's been caught, the walk used to flip at render distance (a3f6be0)

**Stuck runs**

- A phase that runs out of clock with no retries left starts over by itself once, instead of stopping until someone types `#gamer` (1260118)
- A phase takes 8 deaths before it stops instead of 3, the total stays 12 (5a294e7)
- The edge brake works in games started as the idle command too, and catches softer shoves (6e8f6b1)

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs
- `#gamer` is still being worked on and isn't reliable yet. 2 full wins in testing doesn't mean it will win in your world, other recent games still failed in various ways
- Getting through the first Nether trip worked in 11 of 20 test games on recent code, 4 of the last 5 since the dig fix
- A death in the End still costs a long rebuild
- It can still mine while floating in water, which wastes time
- Not in this build, still being worked on: holding near a wither skeleton pack on the way to the blaze spawner, the stall at the end of a lake walk, and the goal marker flickering while exploring
- Vines right next to a far log may still get cut in this build, that fix comes next
- Breaking fire on the spot was only checked against vanilla's server, not against anti-cheat on real servers
- If a run stops with "no progress", type `#gamer` and it picks back up
