### What's new since v1.1.6-anyversion

**Updates**

- Soprano can update itself now. A card in the top left of the title screen says when a new version is out, Update downloads it and it swaps in when you close the game. Turn it off with `checkForUpdates` (95ad884, 596ebe3, 562de94)
- Nightly builds, like this one. When you're up to date the card has a small "Switch to nightly" or "Switch to stable" button, and on a nightly you only get newer nightlies (75e9eac, f729b31, 97ee60a, 5ba50a1)

**Menus**

- New boot and title screens: a black boot screen with the Soprano logo, and the title buttons as a dock. `guiMenus` off gives the vanilla ones back (df33e36, 993215b, f01b180, 276ee3a)
- Menu music, with a mute button in the top right corner. `guiMenuMusic` turns it off too (55ae8b7, 5077b10, c12e5ab, 43c27e4)
- Soft hover and click sounds on the title screen (30b4d50, c38d9a1)
- The click gui sidebar shows the logo instead of the word (12b14c3)

**Schematics**

- The builder leaves piston heads alone instead of breaking and placing the piston forever (edd35bc)
- Powered, lit, extended and other states the redstone sets are no longer "wrong", so built circuits stop getting torn down (75ffff8)

**Baritone and #gamer**

- Bee nests and beehives are never broken by default. Empty `blocksToDisallowBreaking` to get the old behaviour back (9b81174)
- Stopped waiting forever on a dig spot the pathfinder was never going to dig (7cd3d06)
- Never left clicks a block on the never break list, even by accident mid swing (fa20123)
- Ladder clutches don't put a ladder on the wall when the landing is already water (39c27ec)
- After a clutch or MLG into deep water the bot swims back up instead of staying under (648dd26)
- A lit creeper gets baited into popping just out of reach, instead of being walked away from over and over (fd93e96)
- Crafting with a full inventory no longer shuffles junk stacks between slots forever (15227a8)
- The bucket portal tries the next lava lake when the nearest one has no spot for a portal (556ccc9)
- Collecting obsidian skips blocks whose drop would fall into lava (7275590)
- Bats, chickens and rabbits sitting where a pillar block goes get punched out of the way (262fa11)

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs. If something acts weird, report it
- The "Switch to stable" button shows up once v1.1.7 is out. v1.1.6 doesn't have the updater, so going back to it means downloading it from the releases page
