### What's new since v1.1.7-nightly.6

**#gamer is still being worked on**

No full game on this code has beaten the game yet. The best one had 0 deaths and came back from the Nether with rods and pearls at 46 min, then stalled gathering beds until the test's 71 min cap, see Good to know at the bottom. The last full win in testing is still the one on nightly.6's code

**The speedrun portal takes more lakes**

Since these changes it built one in 1 of 5 full games, about 90 s from picking the lake to standing in the Nether. The other 4 gave up on the lakes they found and built the normal portal, so this helps sometimes, not every game

- Walks to a lake it can only half read instead of quitting within a second, two walks per try. It also logs why it gave up now (6c69754)
- Can dig up to 36 blocks for a site and fills up to 2 lava notches in the shore (29aa6df)
- The lake read actually spreads past its first 13 by 13 cells. Set-up lakes: notch about 60 s and far edge about 75 s from the start of the portal phase to the Nether (2dc48ea)
- Builds under a low rock roof and picks a site only once it's standing there. Set-up lakes: low roof about 30 s, cave lake about 55 s, rough shore about 30 s. Gives up after 2 dropped sites or 5 min of searching, walking and clearing, a build already past clearing gets time to finish (91ea44a)

**Water under a low roof**

- Stays standing in water under a roof the server won't let it swim under, that's what kept pinning it at the surface. In the lab with 1 deep water the old code never got out in 10 min, now it has the log in about 15 s. None of the 4 full games since got pinned in water (ae97282)
- A water cell that keeps failing gets sunk out of instead of holding jump. On its own this took that lab from never out to about 7 min (3377566)
- Walking under that roof doesn't swing the crosshair off a gate or a block it's placing. Not seen in a game yet (5afba9a)

**The dragon, tested on set-up fights only**

No full game has beaten the dragon on this code. These fights start in the End with a kit and forced landings

- Keeps clicking after a wing knocks it off the stand, as long as the pillar still covers it and the bed is in reach. Teleported to the floor by the pillar in the test: 8 clicks, dragon 200 to 13 for 2.9 self damage, then credits. The other set-up: 10 clicks, 200 to 31 for 3.8 self damage, then credits, after dying twice to breath on the way to the stand (065d1e1)
- A bed that can finish it off the body doesn't wait for the head. Fired in both fights (065d1e1)
- A breath run keeps going until it's really clear of the cloud (065d1e1)
- Under 10 hp the sword fight waits on the ring until 14, as long as it can heal. Not seen in a game yet (065d1e1)

**Beds and trial chambers**

- Stays away from trial chambers in every overworld phase, not only the first few. In the same world that killed an earlier run on a bed trip: 40 pieces of chamber furniture banned, 0 deaths, closest logged position about 106 blocks from any of them (109f015)
- Beds and wool below y 30 are off the menu, the bot's own beds excepted (109f015)
- Skips the spare room bed when there's none within 100 blocks or no free slot. Not seen in a game yet (109f015)

**The Nether**

- Takes a warped forest off nylium it knows about, not only what it can see. It chose forests this way in 3 full games, and 2 of those finished their pearls. Giving up on a bad forest is not seen in a game yet (91ae5b2)
- Doesn't stand and wait for a wither skeleton pack on the walk to the spawner, no game has shown that hold since. Those packs are still dangerous, see Good to know (61b607e)
- Cooks raw meat right there with the smoker in the bag instead of walking home. Not seen in a game yet (d13ea09)
- Lets go of jump and sprint when a lava hop lands, so it doesn't overshoot back in. Prefers solid ground over magma on a close call. Nothing in the logs shows it either way yet (e42e75a)

**Smaller stuff**

- Doesn't cut a vine on the way to a log that's still out of reach. Lab tower: 10 of 10 vines stay and it gets the log (7e4a4ec)
- The explore goal stays put for up to 10 s, for less path cancelling and flicker. Not seen in a game yet (09e1da1)
- A lake walk that's already there counts as there, instead of doing nothing for 45 s. Covered by a test, not proven in a game (0c2a398)

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs
- Every game above ran on the 26.3 build. This Any Version build has no full game of its own on these changes, it passed the build and its tests and shares the same 26.3 code
- `#gamer` is still being worked on and isn't reliable yet
- Known issue, not fixed in this build: it can stall before the End while gathering beds with a full bag. It says `no room for ...` over and over and `endprep: short by 2 beds`. In the test on this code it sat there from 51 min to the 71 min cap
- The Nether isn't solid. Another test game on nearly this code had 3 deaths, went into the Nether twice without getting what it needed, and was back at iron when it hit the 110 min cap
- Wither skeleton packs can still kill it. In that same game one did, about 30 s after it walked on past the pack
- It can also still die in lava: that game's first death was in a lake while collecting lava for the portal
- A death in the End still costs a long rebuild
- It can still mine while floating in water
- If a run stops with "no progress", type `#gamer` and it picks back up
