### What's new since v1.1.7-nightly.3

**#gamer, round after round**

There were a lot of `#gamer` rounds since nightly.3 (about 150 commits), so here's the short version. Runs get through the Nether and into the End a lot more often than they did

- Fortress and blazes: x-rays fortresses it has loaded, camps blazes out past the spawn area, fights one blaze at a time and drops the ones that drift onto wither skeleton packs (d2f7491, 54fb064, 6118169, b23ab0a)
- Wither skeletons: fights a lone one in contact to the end, stays away from packs and walls off their closets (c0d304b, 254d20c, 8dec873)
- Endermen and pearls: stares calm endermen over from range, shields up when one gets angry, skips the ones by lava or next to brutes, and picks up pearls after kills (c2a0501, 202ecb0, 2f238c6, 53bd8b8)
- Lava: walks lava shores without sprinting, builds a step out when the rim is a wall, surveys lava lakes before walking, and stops the run if the Nether portal lands over a lava lake (b48fd56, 952e3e6, 4b624ca, 418106f)
- Ghasts, magma cubes, creepers and piglins: hits magma cubes first on a rim, steps away from a hissing creeper even mid run, runs from a brute before it swings (721a2b9, 4ace9f9, d199dca)
- Fights with swords instead of axes, carries spare swords and shields, and keeps a shield up in every committed melee fight (6af8b45, b72474b, c0d304b)
- Food and healing: cooks and fetches its food before the Nether, hunts on the way, hides in a hole at night when hurt with nothing to heal with (fbe93a8, 6b0a5d7, 81eb046)
- Eyes and the End: spare eyes and a spare rod so it gets to the portal with eyes left, stops re-throwing once the estimate settles, and End prep survives nights and far bed errands (3514ef2, 5f1fb8d, c7de668)
- Dragon beds got started: crystals first, then the bed goes on the floor under the dragon's head and it clicks when the dragon touches it (b047f89, dd6d548)

**Building and mining**

- `#mine` with a silk touch pick picks the ore block back up instead of leaving it on the ground (3c9737b)
- Schematic builds finish vine columns (cave, weeping and twisting vines too) instead of sitting on them forever (1326c7c)
- Schematic builds can gather terracotta, wood blocks, glass, carpet and dyes now, dyes from real sources like flowers, bone meal and lapis (b76d8e9)
- Doesn't slide off a half built bridge when a task swaps mid step (a7baa27)

**Pathing**

- Path finding skips moves that can never run with your settings and remembers walk-through answers per search, so it does a lot less work per node (a5ded1d)
- Fixed the shared pathing table keeping answers for the wrong settings after a toggle flipped mid search (8a15886)
- `#explore` can't stall in a loaded chunk the cache never got (05b558c)

**Singleplayer and the menus**

- Timers stop counting while singleplayer is paused, so pressing esc no longer fails the current move or times out tasks (30e6830)
- The update card hides itself on narrow windows instead of covering the logo (2846967)
- Only a real rate limit says "rate limit" now, other 403s show the normal failure with the download page (2846967)
- The What's New scrollbar can be dragged, and its update button only closes the screen once the download actually started (2846967)
- A failed update check gets one free retry when the title screen reopens, and resizing the window doesn't use it up (2846967, c1dfd7f)
- The boot logo no longer draws on top of mojang's when another loading screen mod is installed (7d33bc2)

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs
- `#gamer` is still being worked on and probably isn't fully done yet. It has NOT reliably beaten the game from scratch
- It gets to the Nether and gets blaze rods most runs, and gets past the Nether far more often than nightly.3
- It can still die in the Nether (lava, ghast fireballs, wither skeletons) and to the dragon. The dragon bed fight is brand new
- A death in lava means the items are gone and it starts over
- If a run stops with "no progress", type `#gamer` and it picks back up
