### What's new since v1.1.7-nightly.2

**#gamer nether portal**

- New lava pool portal, the speedrun build. With a water bucket and an empty bucket it builds the portal in about 30 seconds (89833b8)
- Walks straight to lava it already knows about instead of waiting for the tracker to hand it some, and goes for surface lava first (438eced, 492d3c1)
- Digs out bumps, low ceilings and banks around the pool and fills small gaps on the shore (2fea094)
- Frames the client already has loaded are walked to directly (ae2ed7b)
- Also sleeps through the night while it is still looking for a portal spot (0e70efb)

**Fighting, one target at a time**

- Fights a lone wither skeleton or skeleton to the end instead of running in circles (6fe6467, 18bf36f)
- Stands and swings at endermen instead of chasing or running (da89657)
- Steps away from any close creeper and sprints clear. The creeper bait mode is gone (245e69f, 9ec0bad)
- Eats mid fight when nothing can hit it, and eats whenever it is hurt and under the regen line (1fe5521, 1270ee4)
- Sticks to one kind of food per bite (6fe6467)
- Blaze fights go one blaze at a time instead of hiding from all of them (c3c24bf)

**Blocks and digging**

- Throwaway digs no longer reserve their own blocks. That was a deadlock where it could not place anything (400d856)
- The nether dig runs for the whole nether trip, and digs walls instead of the floor it stands on (4f6c90e, fad9a85)
- One consistent block count, and placing costs more in the nether so it walks around instead of bridging everything (a501dcb)
- Stronghold digs go in 8 block segments and stronghold corridors are walked instead of tunnelled (60d5871, 3dbf174)

**Gold, food and the nether**

- Mines gold while getting iron and wears a gold helmet before the nether so piglins leave it alone (da89657)
- Goes into the nether with about 210 food instead of 130, so it rarely walks home for more (f3acc45)
- The fortress sweep stops burning search cells while stuck (fad9a85)
- Pearl hunt: uses the plain hunt, the hiding nook is off by default (`altoPearlNook`), steps away from lava, and does not wait forever on an enderman (9be7b9b, 100264d, a2b1966)

**Smaller stuff**

- Fixed a wool hunt loop that froze the iron phase (a501dcb)
- No more descend to ascend sprint shortcut near lava or in the nether (797d074)
- Dragon fight: beds go down late and away from the dragon, and the water bucket is back in the end prep (dc40a06, d94085d)

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs
- `#gamer` has NOT finished a full run from scratch yet in testing
- It reliably gets to the nether in about 17 to 35 minutes, finds the fortress and gets blaze rods
- The nether stretch, mostly the ender pearl hunt, is where runs still die or stall: ghast fireballs, lava, wither skeletons, sometimes getting boxed in
- A death in lava means the items are gone and it starts over
- Night fights on open ground against several skeletons can still kill it
- The stronghold, end and dragon work in isolated tests (dragon killed with beds) but have not been reached in a full run yet
- If a run stops with "no progress", type `#gamer` and it picks back up
