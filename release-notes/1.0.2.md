- `#gamer` shows a status panel on the right of the screen while it runs (c163a7a0d)
- Overworld fights are less indecisive: it ignores mobs until one matters, then fights it out or runs (44e7adf95)

- Walks up straight stairs instead of hopping each step, and keeps sprinting (6aa36e406)
- Melee crits actually land now (e69fad8d7)
- `experimentalMovement` is now called `fastMode`; the old name still works (e825c6788)
