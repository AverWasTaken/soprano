### What's new since v1.1.7-nightly.13

**#gamer: dying in the Nether**

- Before going into the Nether, the bot now puts a bed down next to the overworld portal and sets its spawn there. Dying in the Nether brings it back next to the portal instead of at the world spawn, so it doesn't have to walk all the way back first (8f08319)
- If a bed is already standing by the portal, it uses that one again. If not, it takes one from its bag, and with no bed in the bag it crafts one from the wool it carries. Every way back into the Nether does this again, so a bed it slept in on a food trip doesn't leave its spawn somewhere else (8f08319)
- The bot's own bed collecting leaves the bed by the portal alone. Before the End, it gets one more bed for the dragon fight to make up for it (8f08319)
- It won't put that bed anywhere with lava right next to it (8f08319)
- "Next to the portal" now counts height too. In a test, it once put the bed in a valley about 130 blocks below a portal up on a cliff and counted that as next to the portal (0511709)
- In 4 test games, 5 of its 6 Nether deaths came back next to the portal. Once, the bed was gone a second after the bot clicked it on its way back in, and that death came back at the world spawn. We haven't found out why yet

**#gamer: walking into the End portal**

- The bot now walks to a block in the middle of the End portal, not the closest one. The closest one could be on the edge, where the bot can stand on the frame without ever falling in. One test game gave up right there, at the door to the End (d9bdf23)
- If it still ends up standing over the edge for 3 seconds, it walks itself into the middle. The same goes for the exit portal after the dragon (d9bdf23)
- In a test that put the bot on the edge, the old code got stuck 2 times out of 2 and the new code went in 5 times out of 5

**#gamer: ender pearl hunt**

- After 15 minutes without an ender pearl, the bot could give up the whole run. Now it gives up on that warped forest instead (and everything within 128 blocks of it), looks for another one and starts the 15 minutes over there. With no forest found yet, it keeps hunting and the clock starts over (316da41)
- If it already has the fewest pearls it can make do with, it still stops hunting and moves on, like before (316da41)
- A new forest always gets the full 15 minutes, even when it's found late in the count (5eec35f)

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs
- `#gamer` is still being worked on and isn't reliable yet. 4 test games have finished on this nightly's code so far and none of them beat the game. Two got to the dragon fight: the dragon killed one and it never made it back, the other ran out of its 150 minutes 9 minutes into the fight. The other two never got past the Nether: one gave up after dying there 3 times, one ran out of its 150 minutes. Counting older builds too, it has won about 3 of its last 28 test games
- Both games that reached the End portal walked in (one in 16 seconds). The walk in from the edge never had to kick in during any test. The exit portal change hasn't run in a game yet, since no test game on this build has killed the dragon
- Coming back by the portal hasn't made a Nether death cheap yet. The bot still loses its gear, and in these games it took 36 to 52 minutes to get back into the Nether after a death, bed or no bed. Whether the bed saves time overall hasn't been measured yet
- The new pearl hunt rule has only been tried by replaying a saved game with a 2 minute clock. There it gave up on 2 forests in a row and found a third. It hasn't come up in a full game yet
- Nothing in this nightly changes how the mod loads
- If a run stops and says so, type `#gamer` and it picks back up
