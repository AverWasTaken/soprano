### What's fixed

**Schematics**

- Build in the Schematics tab said "Could not start building" on the release jars, and Move, Rotate and Flip didn't stick. The placements file couldn't be saved at all. Fixed (8d597ad)
- When a build can't start, it now says why (f228ec5)
- `#build <file>` builds where you placed it in the Schematics tab, turned and flipped the same way. `#build` on its own lists what you placed (04a132d)
- The Build button says where it's going to build before you click it again (f228ec5)
- USAGE.md has a short how-to for building a schematic (c9407da)

**#gamer**

- Stopped standing still with the goal flickering after climbing up to smelt and ending up in a cave under a hill (5b6b070)
- Stopped swapping items back and forth forever with a full inventory, and stopped getting stuck on "Putting down the held item" when crafting with a full bag (0739b3e, e6e6c81, 54320e0)
- After a death it puts the armor from the pile on, instead of picking up and throwing away blocks forever (31779f8, 1afc29a)
- Lava portal: stands a block further back so the lava stops landing on it, and gets out right away if lava does reach it (e4265ec, b9ab235, 6b8cc7d)
- Blazes: stopped flipping between fighting and hiding, and stopped freezing up at the spawner (b78b76a)
- Water clutch: picks the water back up after landing, even with a second water bucket in the bag (b377581, 2ff6ec6)

**Baritone**

- Falls with a water bucket pick the water back up when you land a block off or stand still in it (7d709d1, a320bec)
- The bot sprints again on old servers when ViaFabricPlus is installed (07635e2)

**Good to know**

- None of this got play-tested on every version yet. If something acts weird, report it
