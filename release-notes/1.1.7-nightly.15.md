### What's new since v1.1.7-nightly.14

**#gamer: keeping the bed by the Nether portal**

- The bed the bot puts down by the overworld portal could be gone a second after it clicked it, most likely dug through by its own walk to the portal. Nothing told the pathing that the bed was the bot's own, and a bed only takes a moment to break. Now the bot's own pathing and mining leave that bed alone while it stands (b8fe467)
- With no bed in the bag and none standing by the portal, the bot now crafts one from its wool on every way into the Nether, as long as it also has the wood for it (it won't go get any). Before, it only checked for that at one spot on the way in. If the bed went missing after that check, the bot went into the Nether without one, with a pile of wool in the bag (b8fe467)
- In a test where the bed sat in the only gap of a wall between the bot and the portal, the old code broke the bed and the next death came back at the world spawn. The new code walked around the wall 2 times out of 2 and the death came back by the portal

**#gamer: dying in the Nether**

- Going back to gather gear after a Nether death no longer counts toward the back and forth limit. That limit ends a run that keeps bouncing between two steps without getting anywhere, but it was also ending runs on the third Nether death. The death limits still end a hopeless run (b8fe467)

**#gamer: filling a bucket with water**

- A water source with farmland or a dirt path on top looks open, but the bucket can't reach the water through the top. The bot could stand on it saying "no path found" until the whole run gave up. Now, after 12 seconds next to a source without filling the bucket, it gives up on that one source and goes to another (be20e31)
- In a test on both kinds of covered water, the old code stood there for 5 minutes with an empty bucket. The new code moved to the next source after 12 seconds and filled the bucket, 2 tests out of 2

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs
- `#gamer` is still being worked on and isn't reliable yet. On this nightly's code, 5 of 6 test games on the same worlds have finished so far. 3 of them beat the game with 0 deaths (in 80, 95 and 126 minutes). One ran out of its 150 minutes, and one gave up putting the eyes in because the stronghold sits under open ocean with no shore close by. The same 6 worlds won 1 of 6 games in each of the last two test rounds. That looks better, but it's only a handful of games, and none of the 3 wins needed the death or water fixes, so some of it may be luck
- One more game on this code isn't in that count. It ran out of its 150 minutes with 8 deaths while a build was running on the test machine, so it was thrown out and its world was played again (the replay is the 126 minute win). 2 of the 3 wins also had a build running next to them and were kept, since a build can only slow a game down. The two older rounds threw those out either way, so the comparison leans toward this one
- 2 of the counted games died in the Nether once each. One came back by the portal bed. The other never had a portal bed, since it had wool but no wood to craft one, and came back at the world spawn. The one that came back by the bed took 52 minutes to get back into the Nether and the one from the world spawn took 38, so the bed hasn't made a Nether death cheap yet
- Both of those deaths sent the bot back to gather without counting toward the back and forth limit. Neither game got to a third Nether death, so the case that used to end runs hasn't come up in a full game yet
- Crafting the bed on a way back in has only been seen working in one test, never in a full game. In the one game with a portal bed and a death, the bed was still standing on the way back in, but only the wall test shows the bot would have dug through it before
- The covered water fix didn't come up in these games. It's only been seen working in the test above
- Nothing in this nightly changes how the mod loads
- If a run stops and says so, type `#gamer` and it picks back up
