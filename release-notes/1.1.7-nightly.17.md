### What's new since v1.1.7-nightly.16

**#gamer: strongholds under the ocean**

- When the stronghold sits under open ocean with no dry land within 100 blocks of it, the bot used to float over it until its time ran out. Now it falls back to dry land it has stood on, up to 200 blocks out, digs a shaft down from that shore to about the stronghold's depth and tunnels under the sea floor from there. If there's no shore it remembers within 200 blocks, it says so and stops instead of bobbing on the waves (651d082)
- The tunnel goes in short legs of about 14 blocks, each at the right depth for where it is, and tries deeper and to both sides before it calls the tunnel blocked. If the stronghold turns up partway, the tunnel turns toward it. A beach that didn't work is skipped as a whole, so it won't pick the same sand three times, and it won't dig down from a boat over open water. The stop message says which part failed: no shore, never got under the sea floor, the tunnels never got there, or blocked (046cde8)
- In 2 tests that start on the nearest shore, 163 blocks from a stronghold under open ocean, the bot tunnelled the whole way under the sea floor without a boat ride and beat the game, in 12 and 13 minutes. A replay of the nightly.15 test game that gave up on a stronghold under the ocean tunnelled 192 blocks, opened the portal and got into the End. It then died to the dragon's breath and never got ready to go back, which is a separate problem. A replay of a game with its stronghold on land played like before

**#gamer: frozen ponds**

- Ice over water didn't count as a cover for the bucket, so the bot stood on a frozen pond trying to scoop through the ice until it gave up on every water block. Now ice counts as a cover: the bot breaks it, standing on the next block of ice or the shore if it has to, and scoops the water the broken ice leaves right away (af5b656)
- In a test on a frozen pond, the bot had a water bucket in about 7 seconds starting on the ice and about 4 seconds starting on the shore. The older bucket tests, lava from an open pool and water with one covered source next to an open one, played like before

**#gamer: lava while hunting endermen for pearls (Discord #10)**

- The pearl hunt remembers a lava place to keep away from, but it only kept one at a time. With a second lava place nearby, it couldn't mark that one too, so it kept walking back to it, backing off and walking back again until the first one wore off. Now it remembers up to 3, each wearing off on its own (bbdfc37)
- No test reproduced the loop, so this is only checked with unit tests

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs
- `#gamer` is still being worked on and isn't reliable yet. On this nightly's code, 3 of 6 test games on the same worlds beat the game: in 84 minutes with 0 deaths, 115 minutes with 1 death and 119 minutes with 2 deaths. The last two nightlies' code also won 3 of 6 on these worlds, so it held steady. All 6 games counted, with no build running next to any of them
- 2 of the 3 losses ran out of their 150 minutes without getting past the Nether, with 6 and 1 deaths. The third is the world that gave up at a frozen pond last time. This time it broke the ice and filled its bucket in a few seconds and got to the Nether. It died there after an hour, then made no progress for 45 minutes back in the Overworld, so the test stopped it, with 3 deaths in all
- The ocean tunnel never ran in these 6 games. The one world with a stronghold under the ocean went to a different stronghold on land this time and won
- In the test games the pearl hunt marked a lava place 43 times (1 to 15 per game). 21 of those came while an earlier place was still marked, which the old code would have refused. The games don't show whether the loop would have happened without the fix
- A fix for running into wither skeletons down Nether corridors is being tested and isn't in this nightly
- Nothing in this nightly changes how the mod loads
- If a run stops and says so, type `#gamer` and it picks back up
