### What's new since v1.1.7-nightly.15

**#gamer: taking the bed back after a night (Discord #9)**

- With a full bag, the bot often couldn't pick its bed back up after sleeping and left it behind. Clearing the spot for the bed drops blocks on the ground, and they took the slot the bed had just freed. In our test games about 1 in 5 night beds got left like that. Now the bot makes room for the bed the way it does for any other pickup: junk, a worse spare tool or other things the run doesn't need go first. With none of that, it throws spare building blocks, keeping at least 32, and nothing else. During a schematic build it won't throw building blocks either, since those belong to the build. If there's still no room, it leaves the bed and moves on right away instead of waiting out the clock (ea1b96a, 0e49776)
- In a test with a full bag holding junk, the bot threw out netherrack and the bed wasn't left behind. In a test with no junk, it threw 20 dirt, kept its 36 cobblestone and the bed wasn't left behind

**#gamer: lava edges in the Nether**

- Standing still on a lava edge with a hoglin or a melee mob within 8 blocks, the bot now walks to safe ground first, when there is some in reach, and waits there up to 10 seconds while the mob is close (768320d, 1cdce46)
- A drop that ends in lava now counts as dangerous even when the lava is far below. In the Nether the bot won't walk up to a mob to fight it while it or the mob stands next to such a drop, it waits for the mob to come to it. A ledge high above a lava lake counts as a lava shore too, so the bot walks off it and won't stop next to one. It also won't stare down an enderman from a Nether edge anymore (768320d)
- In a test on a 1 block wide bridge over lava with magma cubes, the new code fell in once per run, the old code 2 and 3 times. That's 2 runs each, and the new step found nowhere to go on the bridge, so some of that may be luck. In a test with a hoglin, the bot walked 3 blocks back from the edge about a second before the hoglin's first hit. In a test with endermen on a ledge over lava, the new code killed 2 of 3 on the ledge, the old code 1 of 3

**#gamer: creepers that never light**

- A creeper sitting still a few blocks off the route without lighting, say in a pit or behind a fence, could catch the bot in a loop: step away, walk back past it, step away again, then run 24 blocks, which took it straight back. Now, if a creeper still hasn't lit and has barely moved by the second step, the bot leaves it alone and walks on. If it lights, moves off its spot or gives chase, the bot steps away or runs like before (207a00d)
- In a test with a creeper close enough to light, the bot stepped away once, then left it and walked on. The test that was meant to show the old loop never got the old code stuck either, so the loop itself hasn't been seen fixed in a test

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs
- `#gamer` is still being worked on and isn't reliable yet. On this nightly's code, 3 of 6 test games on the same worlds beat the game: in 73 minutes with 1 death, 84 minutes with 0 deaths and 107 minutes with 2 deaths. The last nightly's code also won 3 of 6 on these worlds, so it held steady. The wins weren't all on the same worlds: one world that won last time ran out of time this time, and the world that gave up on a stronghold under the ocean last time won
- 2 of the 3 losses ran out of their 150 minutes in the Nether, with 7 and 5 deaths. The third gave up filling a water bucket at a frozen pond while getting water to build its Nether portal. A fix for that is being tested and isn't in this nightly
- One more game on this code isn't in that count. It ran out of its 150 minutes while a build was running on the test machine, so it was thrown out and its world was played again. The 107 minute win also had a build running next to it and was kept, since a build can only slow a game down
- In all the test games on the last nightly's code, the bot left its bed behind after 10 of 64 nights. On this code it was 1 of 57, and that one had 8 free slots and couldn't see where the bed had dropped, so it's a different problem. The case with no junk, where building blocks get thrown, didn't come up
- The creeper part fired twice in one game, both times on a creeper that lit right after, so the bot stepped away like before. The step off a lava edge with a mob close never fired
- The other lava changes don't write a line of their own. The bot's walk off a lava shore before a fight, which now also counts ledges over lava, showed up far more in the logs than on the last nightly (474 lines in 7 games against 120 in 8), almost all of it in 3 games. The logs don't say whether the new rule set it off or whether it helped
- The loop where the bot keeps backing off from lava while hunting endermen for pearls (Discord #10) isn't fixed in this nightly. A fix is being tested
- Nothing in this nightly changes how the mod loads
- If a run stops and says so, type `#gamer` and it picks back up
