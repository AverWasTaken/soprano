### What's new

- Neo jumps: hop around the end of a wall and land a few blocks past it, instead of walking the long way round (5dc28dff4)
- Momentum jumps: run up and hop across gaps a standing start can't make (e7af8f9da)
- One switch for a speedrunner-style bot, the `experimentalMovement` setting (58900cb04)
- Rows a boat across lakes instead of swimming or detouring, with `allowBoats` (f797ded13)
- Smoother path drawing, with rounded corners and a fading tail (7588aa5f0)

The new movement tricks are off until you turn them on with `#set`.
