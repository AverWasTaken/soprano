### What's new and fixed

**New stuff**

- Schematic builder: open the click gui (Right Shift or `#gui`), go to the Schematics tab, pick a file from your `schematics` folder and see what blocks it needs (87b85f4)
- Reads Sponge (.schem) and Litematica (.litematic) files (285f449)
- Ghost preview of the build in the world. Move it, turn it and flip it before you start (97ed020, 36f8b18, 0e91e78, 95ec969)
- In survival it gathers the materials first, sleeps through the night, and asks before going to the Nether for any Nether blocks the build needs (9145eab, 31ac2d1, 7636f8d)
- Works in creative too, it hands itself the blocks (8e8e10b)
- Auto torch: places torches in dark spots while mining, walking or doing tasks. Off by default (cc35eb6)
- Self defense: when a mob hits it during `#mine`, `#tunnel`, `#build`, `#farm`, `#follow` or `#goto`, it pulls out its best sword or axe, fights back, then swaps back to what it was holding. On by default (479cf45)
- When a path would be a lot faster if it collected some blocks first, it asks you. Answer with `#gather yes` or `#gather no`, or click Yes/No in chat (c912659)
- `#get` with several ores mines them as one job, nearest first (27d44b0)
- `#follow players` sticks to one player instead of flipping between them (f3ec61d)
- Opens bastion chests for smithing templates instead of breaking them (4b9672a)
- Raises its shield when two mobs pile on it (2b07f4c)
- Stops running away from mobs that keep hitting it and fights back (d0c7c50)

**Fixes**

- Builder: hangs still on ladders, no more sneak spam, stops fighting stairs, fences and its own scaffolding, and stops retrying stuck spots forever (502820e, 029f156, 179e89c, e954fe6)
- Builder: scaffold stays up until the build is done, then gets taken down (7eaf430)
- Builder: chests join into double chests, and doors and gates it walked through stop getting broken and placed again (78e2a0b, 2f288e1)
- Pathing: better on ladders and stairs, and moves that just failed get routed around instead of retried (4f95912, 7c75d43, 09a4185)
- Gravel or sand that slid in front of a block gets broken first (368441c)
- Nether portal builder picks spots it can actually reach (f89e82e)
- Gets a diamond pickaxe before going through the portal (6b10803)
- Blaze hunting goes to the blazes instead of camping (5edec32)
- Lava: being on fire on dry land no longer sends it swimming through lava, and cast lava only gets poured into a clear spot (754472e, b4e347d)
- Swims up out of 2 deep water instead of pillaring (b651477)
- Braking in the middle of a sprint jump lets go of W or holds S, instead of spinning around (fbee69e)
- Get tab stops showing aliased items twice (8edfbe5)
- Fetches a whole wood job in one trip and stops throwing away a good pickaxe (4f76d0d, d86a818)

**How to turn it on**

- Auto torch: `#autoTorch true` (or `#set autoTorch true`). Keep torches in your hotbar. `autoTorchLightLevel` (default 7) and `autoTorchRadius` (default 4) tune it
- Self defense is already on. `#selfDefense false` turns it off
- Never want to be asked about collecting blocks: `#askToGatherForPath false`
- When a build asks about the Nether: `#schem nether yes` or `#schem nether no`

**Good to know**

- Not every new feature got play-tested on every version. If something acts weird, report it
