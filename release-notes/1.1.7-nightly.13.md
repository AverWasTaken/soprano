### What's new since v1.1.7-nightly.12

**#gamer: blaze fights**

- When a blaze is right next to the bot (within about 4.5 blocks) and it has one to hit, it now stands and keeps swinging until it's down to 8 health, instead of running at 14. Running from a blaze that close used to take more hits than it saved. With nothing in reach to hit, it still runs at 14 like before (f4909ca)
- It goes for the most hurt blaze in reach first, else the closest one, and only switches to another close blaze when that one has 5 less health. Every kill is one less blaze shooting at it (f4909ca)
- The same goes for blazes that catch up with it after it has its rods (f4909ca)
- The log now has a line for every swing at a blaze, every hit that lands, and a line when the bot stands in reach with a swing ready and doesn't swing, so a lost blaze fight can be looked at later (f4909ca)
- In a test setup with waves of blazes, the bot cleared 6 of 12 waves instead of 3, with no deaths before or after

**#gamer: Nether food trips**

- When the bot runs out of food in the Nether and goes back to the overworld to hunt, it now tries to hunt up to the full amount of food its plan asks for. Before, it turned back well short of that and often had to leave again a few minutes later. Trips can take longer now, but the time limit on a trip hasn't changed, and a trip that hits it still comes back short (0f174d4)

**Good to know**

- This is a nightly. Expect bugs
- `#gamer` is still being worked on and isn't reliable yet. 6 test games have finished on this nightly's code and 2 of them beat the game: one in about 1 hour 49 minutes with no deaths, one in about 2 hours 9 minutes with 1 death. Of the other 4, two stopped by themselves near the end (one walking into the end portal, one putting the eyes in the portal) and two ran out of their 150 minutes (one still in the Nether after 4 deaths, one a minute into the dragon fight). Counting older builds too, it has won about 3 of its last 22 test games
- The blaze death that started the blaze change, the bot dying right next to the blazes while running away, never happened in the test setup, because the test blazes mostly back off and shoot from a distance. In a full test game with the change, the bot got its 9 rods in about 7 minutes with no deaths. That game later ran out of time hunting ender pearls
- Whether the longer food trips save time overall hasn't been measured yet
- Nothing in this nightly changes how the mod loads
- If a run stops and says so, type `#gamer` and it picks back up
