# What Soprano adds

Everything below is off by default unless it says otherwise. Turn things on in game with `#set <setting> true`, for example `#set allowNeos true`.

- **Neo jumps** (`allowNeos`). Jump around the end of a wall that's 1 or 2 blocks thick and land 2 to 4 blocks out. It runs up first, and bunny hops on the run up if the jump needs the speed. The path is drawn bending around the wall.
- **Momentum jumps** (`allowMomentumJumps`). Jumps a standing start can't make, using a run up and bunny hops: a 4 block gap on the flat, or one up across a gap of 3 (both want 3 or more blocks of run up behind you), drops of 1 to 3 blocks that reach 5 or 6 blocks out, and a few pairs of jumps through a one block pad. Needs `allowParkour`, which `fastMode` turns on for you.
- **Ladder and vine jumps** (`allowClimbJumps`). Catch a ladder or vine mid air across a gap of 1 to 3 blocks, or leap off one onto a ledge or another ladder.
- **Ladder clutches** (`allowLadderClutch`). Survive a long fall by placing a vine or ladder next to where you land, like a water bucket clutch. You need one on your hotbar. `pickupLadders` (on by default) picks the ladder back up afterwards. `fastMode` turns it on.
- **God bridging** (`allowGodBridge`). When the only thing to place against is the block you're standing on, it turns around and walks backwards at walking speed, with no sneaking and no stopping, and places each block the moment it can. A long bridge is one continuous walk instead of sneak, place, sneak. If it can't click in time it sneaks and finishes the block the old way. Not used on ice, honey, in water, on ladders, or on blocks that open when clicked. It's riskier than the sneaking bridge: a placement that doesn't register is a fall, and laggy servers and strict anticheats won't like it.
- **Fast mode** (`fastMode`). Moves more like a speedrunner and less like a robot. It turns on all the parkour (placing a block mid jump too), sprint jumping, head hitters, god bridging, ladder clutches, swimming, boats and elytra takeoff from the ground, takes falls that cost some health when that saves time (it stays above `fastModeMinHealth`), cuts corners, smooths out straight runs, places blocks more freely, and leans toward jumps.
- **Boats** (`allowBoats`) and better swimming (`allowSwimming`).
- **AltoClef built in.** Ask for an item and it works out how to get it: mining, crafting, smelting, fighting and eating along the way. `#get iron_pickaxe`, `#food 20`, or `#gamer` to try and beat the game. It does nothing until you give it a task.
- **Path rendering.** The path is a smooth antialiased ribbon with rounded corners, and the search visual is softer.
- **Costs that match the game.** The planner prices moves in ticks, and a few of those prices were off, so it picked slower routes. Water is priced at wading speed (about twice the old price, so lakes are detoured round more), soul sand at the 58% speed it gives and not 50%, with Soul Speed boots taking the slowdown off (priced as plain walking, and not yet when stepping off it down a block), falls onto ground at the drop they really are, corners edged round at a walk, stairs walked up the way they face with no jump, and a block that takes several ticks to break at whole ticks plus the wait before the next one (`blockBreakAdditionalPenalty` is still just the tiebreaker on top). Drops are estimated at what a fall costs and not what a staircase does: the cheapest a block of falling gets without a bucket or a ladder, counting the health a fall past 3 blocks takes. A cheaper guess made digging down to something far below look like it went nowhere, so it said "No path found".
- **Pathfinder performance.** More movements per second, and the cache buffers get reused instead of reallocated.
- **Movement timings** (`recordMovementTimings`, off by default). Measures how many ticks every movement really takes against what the planner said, and keeps a small capped file of it. `#timings` shows which movements are mispriced the most. It only measures, nothing changes how paths are planned.
- **Asks before collecting blocks** (`askToGatherForPath`, on by default). On a trip you started (`goto`, `come`, a waypoint, click to go), when it can't get there without blocks you don't have it asks "may I collect some so I can continue?" in chat with clickable Yes and No. It also asks when a route that needs blocks or a stone pickaxe is at least 30% and 30 seconds faster, counting the time to collect them. Once per trip, and the answer holds for the whole trip.
- **Chat prefix.** Messages say `[Soprano]`.

The new stuff is new, so expect rough edges. [USAGE.md](USAGE.md) lists the settings that go with these. [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md) has the full list of what the pathfinder can do. Bug reports and feedback are welcome.
